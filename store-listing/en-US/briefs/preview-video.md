# Brief: Preview video (Bomp — en-US) — optional

## Play Store spec
- YouTube URL (public or unlisted).
- Horizontal (1920 × 1080), even though the app is vertical — the YouTube player on the listing requires it.
- Monetization off.
- Bomp's own audio playing from second 1 (what the user "buys" is the auditory value).

## 30-second structure

| Seconds | Content | Voice / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Fade in of the `bomp-mark.svg` mark over `Ink1000`. | "Bomp" wordmark; tagline "Voices that matter." in `Paper`. |
| 3–10 | Screen: WhatsApp with an incoming voice note. Tap on the "Share" menu and pick Bomp as the destination. | Caption: **Save** the voice notes that matter. |
| 10–18 | Screen: Add Button screen. The user types a personal name (e.g. "Mom's laugh"). | Caption: **Name** what only you'd get. |
| 18–25 | Main screen with the new sticker. The user taps it and it plays. **They do not** send it to anyone. The face of whoever hears it (off-screen) smiles. | Caption: **Bomp it** when you need it — you, first. |
| 25–30 | Cut to `Ink1000` background with the manifesto. | "A voice from your people isn't a message — it's a hug you can hear." + logo. |

## Rules
- The sticker audio must play starting at second 1 — zero latency is part of the product.
- Zero "Send" / "Share" in captions until it's clear it's optional. The video's primary action is **listening to it for yourself**.
- Zero "audio sticker" analogies in the video copy — coherence with brand-dna §5.

## Production
Out of scope for this delivery. The brief stands as a script for whenever video investment is decided.
