Your favorite voice notes, always with you.

Bomp is where you keep your mom's laugh, the boss's catchphrase, the voice note from a friend that always makes you smile. Your collection. Yours. Yours to play anytime — share when you want to.

— Save. Name. Bomp it. —

▸ SAVE
Grab the voice note that made you laugh (or cry) and drop it into your collection. Doesn't matter if it came from WhatsApp, Telegram, or WeChat — if you love it, save it.

▸ NAME
Make it yours. Give it a name only you'd get — the one that makes you smile when you see it in the list. Bomp won't second-guess you.

▸ BOMP IT
Tap it when you need it. A two-second joke can save the day — yours, first. And if you want, share it: someone on the other end laughs out loud.

— Who Bomp is for —

For people tired of long WhatsApp voice notes who want something short, instant. For people who want the voices that matter close at hand. For people who collect moments the way others collect photos. For tight groups with their own inside jokes that nobody else gets.

— Truly private —

Your voice notes live on your phone. Bomp doesn't upload, analyze, or share them. What you saved stays with you.

— Just open and go —

No signup, no email, no phone number, no account. Download Bomp, open it, and you're bomping. Zero "create your account" screens. Zero "verify your number" steps. The only thing left is picking the first voice to save.

— Your collection sticks with you —

Android quietly backs your collection up to your personal Google Drive — you don't touch a thing. New phone, stolen phone, factory reset, dropped in a lake: install Bomp on the new device and your collection comes back, names and favorites and pins intact. The cloud keeps it for you.

— Your new vocabulary —

Bomper /bom·per/ (say "BOMP-er")
You, right now. The person who collects voices the way others collect hugs.

Bompear /bom·pe·ar/ (say "bomp-ee-ARR")
To activate a Bomp: to play it or share it. First for you, then for others — if you want.

Bompeable /bom·pe·a·ble/ (say "bomp-ee-AH-bleh")
Those voice memos you love and don't want to lose. Short or long. Funny or tender. Useful.
